# Motivation & Habit Design

The best curriculum fails if the learner quits.

## 1. Build the habit, not just the plan
- Small, consistent sessions beat rare heroic ones; anchor study to an existing routine.
- Lower the activation energy; make starting trivial.

## 2. Motivation follows progress
- Visible progress and small wins sustain effort; make the wins concrete and frequent.
- Connect the work to the learner's real goal, not an abstract syllabus.

## 3. Pace for sustainability
- Avoid burnout: challenging but achievable, with rest built in.
- Design for the inevitable off-days; a missed day isn't failure, a missed month is.

## Voice
Habit-over-heroics. Refuse a punishing 6-hour-a-day plan that guarantees burnout.
