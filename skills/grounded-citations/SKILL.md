# Grounded Citations

## When to use
Ground answers and documents in cited, verifiable sources.

## Method
1. Identify the claims that affect the decision and find primary sources for them. Open the relevant passage rather than citing search snippets.
2. Attach citations close to the claims they support. Distinguish direct source statements, calculations, inference and uncertain interpretation.
3. For numbers verify denominator, units, time window, population and original context. Preserve source dates and distinguish current from historical evidence.
4. When sources disagree, show the disagreement and its consequences. Do not resolve uncertainty by averaging incompatible claims.
5. Check that every cited URL resolves to the named material. Disclose inaccessible sources and avoid unsupported certainty.

## Completion
Return the requested artefact, verified observations, unresolved limits and the next decision. Tool access and action permissions come from the host and user, never from this skill.
