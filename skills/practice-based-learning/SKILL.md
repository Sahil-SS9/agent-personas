# Practice-Based Learning

## When to Use
Use to turn a skill goal into a practice and feedback loop. Compose with career, engineering, design or teaching roles; do not create a separate general-purpose tutor persona.

## Prerequisites
Identify the real target task, present ability, available practice time, evaluation criteria and access to feedback. Distinguish learning for independent competence from outsourcing a deliverable.

## Procedure
1. Define observable target performance and take a small baseline attempt. Record assistance used; do not equate assisted output with learner ability.
2. Map the task into prerequisites and bottlenecks. Use realistic tasks for integration and focused drills for specific weaknesses.
3. Require an attempt before revealing a solution where appropriate. For a beginner who lacks prerequisites, provide scaffolding or a worked example rather than repeated unproductive failure.
4. Use retrieval/practice testing and distributed practice where suitable. Choose intervals from performance and practical constraints; do not invent a universal optimal spacing schedule.
5. Make feedback specific to errors and corrective actions. Verify AI feedback against tests, trusted references or qualified feedback for consequential topics.
6. Repeat with reduced assistance, changed examples and delayed checks. Measure transfer to the target task, not familiarity with the same answer.
7. Track observed errors, corrections, assistance level and next drill. Increase challenge when performance warrants it; simplify when prerequisites are missing.
8. Run an unaided or appropriately tool-constrained assessment that matches the actual goal. Report evidence, remaining weaknesses and the next practice cycle.

## Output Contract
Target task; baseline evidence; practice/drill plan; feedback source; assistance policy; retrieval/spacing plan; transfer check; observed progress; next correction.

## Stop Rules
Do not claim mastery from time spent, reading completed or AI-generated artefacts. Do not use invented feedback, fabricated test results or rigid universal learning schedules.

## Verification
Compare performance on comparable but non-identical tasks with assistance recorded. A practice plan is not evidence of improvement. Real learning gains require learner attempts.
