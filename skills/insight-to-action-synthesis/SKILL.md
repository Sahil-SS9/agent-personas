# Insight-to-action synthesis

## When to use
After any research round.

## Procedure
1. Collect evidence verbatim (quotes, observed behaviour, numbers) before interpreting; keep observation and interpretation visually separate.
2. Cluster into findings; attach the strongest evidence to each; mark confidence honestly.
3. Convert each finding to a consequence: what we now believe, what we will do differently, what it costs. Insight without consequence is decoration.
4. State invalidated beliefs explicitly: what we used to think that this research overturned.
5. Date the synthesis; set a review point — research has a shelf life as the product and market move.

## Decision rules
- Every finding names its evidence; every belief names its status.
- Deliver to the decision-maker in their format: the decision, the why, the risk.

## Pitfalls
- Insight decks that change nothing.
- Generalising from one memorable interview.

## Done
A dated synthesis with evidenced findings, explicit consequences, and invalidated beliefs logged.
