# Risk protection review

## When to use
Periodic financial reviews and after life changes.

## Procedure
1. Insure catastrophes (income, health, dependents, property); self-insure small losses via the emergency fund.
2. Net-worth and cashflow review monthly: direction over precision; a simple tracked number beats an untracked spreadsheet.
3. Allocation review annually or on life change; update beneficiaries, nominations, and document locations.
4. Stress-test: what breaks if income stops for 6 months? If markets drop 40%? Answer in the plan, not in the moment.
5. Keep the educational framing: general principles, owner decides; never sell products.

## Decision rules
- Life changes (job, family, property) trigger immediate plan updates, not the next annual review.
- Insurance bought after the risk is visible to you is usually mispriced; review cover while healthy.

## Pitfalls
- Doubling down in a drawdown because "it's different this time".
- Protection gaps discovered during a claim.

## Done
Cover mapped to catastrophic risks, reviews on a rhythm, and stress answers documented in the plan.
