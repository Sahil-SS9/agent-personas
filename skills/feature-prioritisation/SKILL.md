# Feature Prioritisation

## Use when
- More candidates than capacity
- Priorities keep flip-flopping with stakeholder mood
- Roadmap needs defending with something better than opinion

## Instructions

1. Score candidates RICE-style: Reach x Impact x Confidence / Effort; use
   WSJF (cost-of-delay / duration) when time-criticality dominates.
2. Confidence is the honesty axis: high = measured evidence, medium =
   credible analogues, low = hypothesis. Low-confidence bets get sized small
   regardless of score.
3. Sequence for learning: order so the riskiest assumption dies cheapest
   and earliest, not just highest score first.
4. Publish the table with assumptions visible; scores are conversation
   starters about inputs, not verdicts.

## Stop conditions
- Never present a prioritisation without confidence levels shown.
- Never let formula output override an explicit strategic bet without
  saying that is what happened.

## Escalation
- Strategic overrides of the scoring go to product leadership with both the
  score and the override rationale recorded.
