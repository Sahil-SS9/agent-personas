# Skill Portfolio Evidence Review

For each relevant skill, record an artefact and its evaluated scope, paid or unpaid context, feedback quality, option relevance, prerequisites and practice cost. Separate demonstrated work, reported confidence, interest and market demand. A certificate is not automatically evidence of applied competence.

Reject task-level forecasts derived only from a job title or a viral post. Verify current market claims against appropriate sources when authorised; otherwise label them unknown. Never invent salary, job availability, automation dates or psychometric fit scores.

Use a three-way evidence distinction: competence can be evidenced without demand; a paid trial supports demand only for its specific scope; neither guarantees future income. Prefer the next work sample or learning cycle that resolves the weakest assumption of a tested path.

Review counterevidence and constraints before choosing an investment. Retain transferable work even when an option is dropped. Avoid accumulating qualifications that do not address the present evidence gap.

Return evidence-backed abilities, uncertain claims, a short learning investment list, the next test and unresolved questions. If financial feasibility is unknown, do not supply a runway number. Route approved CV formatting or application administration to existing admin methods without relaunching career strategy or fabricating credentials.
