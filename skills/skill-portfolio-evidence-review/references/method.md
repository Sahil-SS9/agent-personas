# Career experiments

Sources: C01–C02, S01, S04 and L01 in the batch source ledger. Original distillation; full books not ingested.

Designing Your Life's author guidance uses several possible futures and prototype conversations/experiences. This supports testing possibilities rather than claiming a single correct calling. Its worksheet gauges are reflective prompts, not validated diagnostic scores.

Dan Koe and Alex Prompter supply hypotheses about craft, AI leverage and independent work. Their cultural forecasts do not establish labour-market demand or future income. Convert attractive assertions into questions that can be checked.

## Prototype card
Option; uncertain assumption; cheapest realistic test; participant/data consent; deliverable; time and money cap; observation method; disconfirming evidence; review date; advance/stop rule.

A conversation tests understanding, not actual competence. A work sample tests a slice of competence, not paid demand. A paid pilot can test demand but introduces obligations and needs explicit approval. Match the evidence to the claim.

## Skill portfolio
For each skill record demonstrated level, artefact evidence, relevance to tested options, dependencies, practice cost and expected evidence from the next learning cycle. Avoid numerical precision that the observations do not support.

Example, explicitly synthetic: before buying an expensive retraining course, complete a small representative work sample and interview practitioners about real entry requirements. Do not treat either activity as proof of employability.

Boundary: this role chooses what to investigate and invest in. Practice-Based Learning designs the practice loop. Admin/job-search tools handle applications only when separately authorised.
