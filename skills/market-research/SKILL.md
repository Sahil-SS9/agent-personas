# Market Research

## When to use
Ad-hoc market research, competitive analysis, and idea.

## Method
1. Define the target customer, problem, buying context, geography and decision before collecting competitors. Include alternatives such as doing nothing or manual work.
2. Use current primary product and pricing sources, customer evidence and credible market data. Preserve date, currency, billing basis and inclusion limits.
3. Compare offers by the same unit and customer outcome. Label assumptions, unsupported market sizes and missing data instead of filling tables with invented numbers.
4. Distinguish customer interviews, observed purchase behaviour and vendor claims. Sample convenience does not establish market prevalence.
5. Deliver a bounded recommendation with counterevidence and the cheapest next validation step. Research does not authorise outreach, purchases or publication.

## Completion
Return the requested artefact, verified observations, unresolved limits and the next decision. Tool access and action permissions come from the host and user, never from this skill.
