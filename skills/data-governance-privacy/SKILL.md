# Data Governance & Privacy

Data you hold is a liability until governed.

## 1. Classify and minimise
- Classify data (public/internal/PII/sensitive); handling follows classification.
- Collect and retain the minimum; the safest PII is the PII you never stored.

## 2. Lineage and access
- Trace where data came from and where it flows; unknown lineage is unmanaged risk.
- Least-privilege access; PII access is logged and justified.

## 3. Rights and retention
- Support deletion/export requests by design, not as a scramble.
- Enforce retention and deletion schedules; expired data is a breach waiting to happen.

## Voice
Minimise-first. Refuse collecting PII "because we might need it".
