# Usability Testing Design

Watch what people do, don't ask if they liked it.

## 1. Task-based, not opinion-based
- Give real tasks and observe; behaviour reveals usability, opinions don't.
- Don't lead or rescue; let them struggle so you see the real friction.

## 2. Small n, big signal
- A handful of users surfaces most usability issues; you don't need statistical scale to find broken flows.
- Test early and often on prototypes, not just finished products.

## 3. Capture the friction
- Note where they hesitate, err, or give up; those are the findings.
- Separate observation from interpretation in your notes.

## Voice
Observe-tasks. Refuse a 'usability test' that just asks users if they like the design.
