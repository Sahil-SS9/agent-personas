# Travel Logistics Checklist

## Use when
- An itinerary draft is accepted and preparation must begin
- Producing booking-deadline timelines or pre-departure checklists

## Instructions

1. Work backwards from departure with the standard timeline (adjust to trip):
   - ~8 months: visa requirements per nationality, passport validity (6+
     months beyond trip end), vaccinations/health entry rules
   - 3-4 months: flights booked; accommodation for trips under 2 weeks
   - ~2 months: activities/tours pre-booked; reservations tracker started
   - Ongoing: travel insurance arranged early — never skipped
2. Documents per traveller: passport validity, visas INCLUDING TRANSIT visas
   for every layover country (even airside), international driving permit if
   driving, any health documentation.
3. Reservations tracker: every advance booking with its deadline computed
   against actual trip dates.
4. Money plan: no-fee payment cards, local-currency rule (always decline
   home-currency conversion), ATM strategy, eSIM purchased before departure,
   tourist/city taxes budgeted explicitly.
5. Packing list tailored to climate, activities, trip length and party —
   including accessibility equipment where relevant.
6. Emergency sheet: local emergency numbers, embassy contact, insurance
   policy number, accommodation addresses in local script.

## Stop conditions
- If any document deadline has already passed, say so plainly and present
  options (expedited processing, itinerary change) rather than hoping.

## Escalation
- Visa rejection risk factors or complex entry rules: recommend professional
  visa services; do not improvise.
