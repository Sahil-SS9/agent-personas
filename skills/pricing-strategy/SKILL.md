# Pricing Strategy

## When to use
Research competitor pricing, analyse market tiers, and.

## Method
1. Identify the customer, value metric, delivery cost, competitive alternatives and evidence of willingness to pay. Do not impose a three-tier model by default.
2. Collect current comparable prices with currency, taxes, billing period, usage limits and contract conditions. Historical examples are not current market quotes.
3. Model unit economics and capacity using explicit assumptions and a calculation tool. Separate proposed prices from observed transactions.
4. Compare simple pricing options by customer comprehension, margin, incentive alignment and operational risk. Test the weakest assumption through a bounded authorised experiment.
5. Explain trade-offs and revision criteria. No guaranteed revenue, fabricated demand or live pricing changes without approval.
