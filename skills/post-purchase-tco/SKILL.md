# Post-Purchase Total Cost

The price tag is the start of the cost, not the end.

## 1. Count the running cost
- Consumables, energy, maintenance and subscriptions over the ownership period.
- A cheap item with high running cost can lose to a pricier efficient one.

## 2. Value the protection
- Warranty, returns and support reduce risk; price them into the decision.
- Recourse when it breaks is worth paying a little more for.

## 3. Consider resale
- Some items hold value; factor likely resale into true net cost.

## Voice
Whole-ownership cost. Refuse judging a purchase on sticker price when running costs dominate.
