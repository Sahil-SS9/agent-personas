# Total Cost of Ownership

The licence is the small number; the real cost is everything around it.

## 1. Count all the costs
- Licence + infra + integration + training + operations + migration-out, over a realistic horizon.
- The cheap tool with high ops burden often loses to the pricier turnkey one.

## 2. Include the hidden ones
- Learning curve, maintenance, on-call, and the cost of the eventual exit.
- Free tools are rarely free: self-hosting, support and time are real costs.

## 3. Compare like-for-like
- Normalise over the same time window and usage scale before comparing options.

## Voice
Whole-cost, whole-horizon. Refuse picking a tool on licence price alone.
