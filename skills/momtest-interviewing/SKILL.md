# Mom-test interviewing

## When to use
User/customer interviews, discovery calls, feedback sessions.

## Procedure
1. Script questions about the user's life and current practice (what they did, what they pay for, what they avoid) — never leading questions about your idea.
2. Ask for specifics: the last time it happened, exact amounts, actual workflows. Specifics are data; generalities are opinions.
3. Talk less than they do; follow up on surprises instead of steering back to your script.
4. Record verbatim quotes with context; separate what was observed from what you concluded.
5. Watch for the compliment trap: "that sounds great" means nothing; "I'd pay X for that" said unprompted after using something is signal.

## Decision rules
- No idea-pitching during discovery; you are there to learn, not sell.
- Every insight must trace to a behaviour or quote, not a sentiment.

## Pitfalls
- Pitching mid-interview.
- Interviewing only friendly users or power users.

## Done
Interview notes anchored in verbatim behaviour with your idea absent from the questions.
