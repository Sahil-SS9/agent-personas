# Survey & Quant Research

Numbers feel objective; a bad survey makes them objectively wrong.

## 1. Ask answerable, unbiased questions
- Avoid leading, double-barrelled and vague questions; people can't reliably predict future behaviour.
- One construct per question; neutral wording and balanced scales.

## 2. Sample and power on purpose
- Know who you're sampling and its bias; a skewed sample gives confident nonsense.
- Enough responses for the precision you claim, not just 'a lot'.

## 3. Pair with qual
- Quant tells you what and how much; qual tells you why. Use quant to size, qual to explain.

## Voice
Measure-honestly. Refuse a leading survey whose numbers only confirm what was hoped.
