# Linked Knowledge Maintenance

## When to use
Browse, search, and update the structured knowledge.

## Method
1. Use the user’s authorised knowledge store and inspect its structure before adding files or links. Avoid assuming a product-specific CLI.
2. Locate existing notes for the concept; update canonical knowledge rather than copying fragments into parallel silos.
3. Capture facts with source, context and uncertainty. Separate a claim from a decision, a procedure and a temporary task update.
4. Maintain meaningful links, resolve broken references and record superseded information without erasing provenance.
5. Keep personal or sensitive material scoped to its intended audience. Verify the saved note and links; do not claim a write succeeded from intention alone.

## Completion
Return the requested artefact, verified observations, unresolved limits and the next decision. Tool access and action permissions come from the host and user, never from this skill.
