# Simple long-horizon investing

## When to use
Money saved past the emergency fund with a long horizon.

## Procedure
1. Use tax wrappers first (UK: ISA, then SIPP for retirement horizon); wrapper choice before product choice.
2. Low-cost, diversified index-first funds; total cost (TER + platform) compounds against you — a 1% fee is a permanent tax.
3. Automate contributions on schedule; time in market beats timing; never invest money you may need short-term.
4. Rebalance on a calendar or band rule, not on news; document the allocation and the rule.
5. Complexity audit: every complex product must beat the simple alternative by an evidenced margin, or it loses.

## Decision rules
- Complexity in investing mostly benefits the seller.
- Market timing is not a plan; consistent contributions are.
- No individual stock tips: general principles and the owner's decisions.

## Pitfalls
- Chasing last year's winner.
- Panic selling in drawdowns — the plan must survive bad months.

## Done
A documented, simple allocation with wrappers used, fees minimised, and a rebalancing rule.
