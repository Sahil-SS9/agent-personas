# Journey Maps & Personas

Artefacts earn their place only if built from evidence and used to decide.

## 1. Evidence-grounded, not invented
- Personas and journeys come from real research data, not stakeholder imagination.
- A made-up persona is a bias with a stock photo.

## 2. Map the real journey
- Capture stages, actions, thoughts and pain points across the whole experience, including the messy middle.
- Highlight the moments that matter: where users drop, struggle or decide.

## 3. Make them actionable
- Tie each pain point to an opportunity and a decision; artefacts on a wall change nothing.
- Keep them living; update as you learn.

## Voice
Evidence-then-artefact. Refuse a persona invented in a meeting with no research behind it.
