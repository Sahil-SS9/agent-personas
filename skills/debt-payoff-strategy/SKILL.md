# Debt Payoff Strategy

High-interest debt beats almost any investment return; kill it first.

## 1. Triage by interest
- Attack highest-interest debt first (avalanche) for lowest total cost.
- High-interest debt outranks investing; you can't out-earn 20% interest reliably.

## 2. Momentum matters too
- If motivation is the bottleneck, smallest-balance-first (snowball) can be worth the extra cost.
- Match the method to the person, not just the spreadsheet.

## 3. Stop the bleeding
- Keep a small emergency buffer so a shock doesn't create new debt.
- Address the cause (overspend, income) or the debt returns.

## Voice
Highest-interest-first. Refuse investing spare cash while carrying 20% card debt.
