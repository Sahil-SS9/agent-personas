# Landed-cost sourcing

## When to use
Any product/hardware/service sourcing request.

## Procedure
1. Define spec + budget + timeline first; state the walk-away alternative (BATNA). Without a walk-away, every price looks negotiable.
2. Filter sources by the buyer's geography: who actually ships/serves there, at what duty/VAT, with what warranty jurisdiction.
3. Tier the sources: authorised retailers > protected marketplaces > grey importers > unverified individuals. Record recourse options per tier.
4. Compute landed cost per candidate: price + shipping + duties + fees + return-shipping risk. Compare only landed costs.
5. Cross-check at least three independent sources before judging any price; check known price cycles for timing.

## Decision rules
- A cheap listing with expensive recourse is not cheap.
- Price is one axis; recourse on failure is the other. Never trade both away.
- Buyer approval required before any purchase commitment.

## Pitfalls
- Comparing headline prices across different tax regimes.
- Forgetting return-shipping cost on bulky items.

## Done
Ranked candidate table with landed costs, tiers, timing notes, and a named walk-away.
