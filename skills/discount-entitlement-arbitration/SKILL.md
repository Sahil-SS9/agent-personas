# Discount entitlement arbitration

## When to use
Sourcing compute cheaply, or reviewing whether an existing discount is being used correctly.

## Procedure
1. Enumerate entitlement routes: education, research, startup schemes, open-source programmes, regional pricing, seasonal promotions, referral and partner credits.
2. Check eligibility honestly against the actual entity or status.
3. Note the obligations each scheme carries: reporting, attribution, non-commercial limits, clawback.
4. Compare the discount against the friction: verification steps, renewals, and the migration cost when it ends.
5. Record expiry and re-verification dates.

## Decision rules
- Read the eligibility terms as a constraint, not as a formality. Misrepresenting eligibility risks the account and the work on it.
- A discount with clawback on misuse is a contingent liability; price it as one.
- Non-commercial terms disqualify commercial use regardless of price.
- Prefer the discount whose expiry you can plan for.

## Pitfalls
- Building on a student or research tier that forbids production use.
- Ignoring that credits expire before they can be consumed.
- Missing renewal paperwork and losing the rate mid-project.

## Done
Each claimed entitlement has verified eligibility, stated obligations, and a recorded expiry.
