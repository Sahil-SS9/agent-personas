# Negotiation BATNA discipline

## When to use
Used-goods, one-off, or bulk purchases where negotiation applies.

## Procedure
1. Prepare the BATNA before first contact: the walk-away price and alternative acquisition path. Your power comes from genuinely having an alternative.
2. Anchor on objective criteria: completed-sale prices for identical items, documented condition issues, market cycle position — not on the asking price.
3. Separate the person from the problem: firm on criteria, polite with people; ask questions before countering.
4. Bundle the ask: price + shipping + extras move together; a concession on one axis can pay on another.
5. Log every offer/counter in the ledger; patterns across sellers inform the next round.

## Decision rules
- Walk away at BATNA: a deal slightly better than a bad one is still bad.
- Never reveal the budget ceiling; negotiate against criteria, not against feelings.
- Time pressure cuts both ways: if the seller is rushed, so is the deal quality.

## Pitfalls
- Anchoring yourself to the first number heard.
- Negotiating items that are commodity-priced anyway.

## Done
Negotiations conducted from a named BATNA with objective criteria and a complete offer ledger.
