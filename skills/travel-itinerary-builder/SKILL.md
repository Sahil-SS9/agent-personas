# Travel Itinerary Builder

## Use when
- Destination and dates are agreed; a day-by-day plan is needed
- Reviewing an existing itinerary for pacing or structural problems

## Instructions

1. Build day-by-day from the research output. Each day gets:
   - ONE named anchor activity + ONE named backup (weather/closure cover)
   - Meals with named venues and the REASON for each pick
   - Realistic transit times between stops — never idealised map estimates
2. Pacing rules (non-negotiable):
   - 30-60 minutes buffer between major activities
   - Maximum 3-4 major attractions per day
   - No heavy sightseeing on arrival day (jet lag)
   - Full-day excursions get their own day — travel time counts
   - Route order without backtracking
3. Narrative rhythm: each day should have an opening, a build, a highlight,
   and a wind-down. An itinerary is an architecture of experience, not a list.
4. Pause-and-verify: present ONE day at a time for approval before building
   the next. Never dump a whole trip at once.
5. Party-aware adjustments:
   - Multi-generational: slower pace, accessible rooms verified, nap/quiet
     windows, excursions that work for youngest AND oldest
   - Accessibility: step-free routes mapped in advance, one major morning +
     one afternoon, rest breaks built in
6. Risk pass: for every day note bottlenecks, weather exposure and closure
   days, each with its backup already named.

## Stop conditions
- If the traveller requests more than pacing allows (e.g. 12 cities in 10
  days), push back with a better alternative instead of complying silently.

## Escalation
- Conflicts between party members' needs: present trade-offs explicitly.
