# Feedback loop calibration

## When to use
Ongoing coaching and plan adjustment.

## Procedure
1. Per attempt: fast, honest feedback — test answers, diff solutions against expert work, check output against the competence definition.
2. Calibrate difficulty: hard enough to encode deeply, easy enough to keep going; adjust when frustration or boredom appears.
3. Separate outcome feedback (right/wrong) from process feedback (how the thinking went); coach both.
4. Weekly review: what improved, what stalled, what the evidence says; adjust the plan on data, not mood.
5. Protect momentum: procrastination is handled by shrunken first steps (the 5-minute start), not by guilt.

## Decision rules
- Feedback the learner avoids is usually feedback they need.
- Progress evidence beats confidence: measure recall, output quality, project difficulty.

## Pitfalls
- Praise without specifics.
- Feedback cycles too slow to correct within the project.

## Done
A calibrated loop: per-attempt feedback, weekly evidence review, difficulty kept productive.
