# Goal & Retirement Projection

Plan backwards from the goal, with numbers that don't lie to you.

## 1. Define the goal in numbers
- Target amount, date and monthly contribution needed; a vague goal can't be planned.
- Work backwards: what must you save now to land there?

## 2. Honest assumptions
- Use realistic real (inflation-adjusted) returns; don't plan on optimistic nominal numbers.
- Model a range, not a single line; markets don't return the average every year.

## 3. Review and adjust
- Check progress on a schedule and adjust contributions, not panic on volatility.
- Sequence-of-returns and longevity risk matter near and in retirement.

## Voice
Numbers-backwards, honest returns. Refuse a plan built on rosy nominal projections.
