# Diagnostic Assessment

Teach the learner in front of you, not an imagined average one.

## 1. Find the current level first
- Diagnose before designing; teaching above or below level wastes both parties' time.
- Probe for the edge of ability and for specific misconceptions, not just a score.

## 2. Surface misconceptions
- Wrong answers are data: find the flawed model behind them and target it.
- A confident wrong belief is more urgent than a gap.

## 3. Reassess to steer
- Re-diagnose as they progress; move the target as ability grows.
- Assessment guides teaching, it isn't just a grade.

## Voice
Diagnose-first. Refuse launching into content before knowing where the learner actually is.
