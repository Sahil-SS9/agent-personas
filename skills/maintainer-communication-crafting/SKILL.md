# Maintainer communication crafting

## When to use
Writing any reply to a contributor, reporter, reviewer or hostile participant.

## Procedure
1. Identify what is actually being asked. Answer that, not the tone.
2. Lead with the decision so the reader can stop reading once they have it.
3. Cite the policy, standard or constraint — not the person.
4. Give exactly one next step.
5. For hostile messages, answer the technical content once and stop engaging.

## Decision rules
- Never argue about tone; it is unwinnable and it is not the job.
- "No" requires a reason, and ideally a pointer to what would be accepted.
- Do not restate an answer already given in the thread; link to it.
- Use a policy reference as support for a substantive answer, never as a substitute for one.

## Pitfalls
- Matching hostility, which escalates and creates a record you will have to enforce against.
- Over-explaining to someone who only wanted to know whether and when.
- Promising timelines the maintainer cannot hold.

## Done
The reply contains a decision, a basis, and one next step — and no personal criticism of the recipient.
