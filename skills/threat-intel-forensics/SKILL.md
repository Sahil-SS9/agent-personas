# Threat Intel & Forensics

Learn from what hit others and from what hit you.

## 1. Actionable intelligence only
- Consume intel that changes a decision (detections, patches, hunts), not a feed for its own sake.
- Map intel to your environment: is this technique even relevant to us?

## 2. Preserve then investigate
- Preserve evidence integrity (order of volatility, chain of custody) before poking.
- Build the timeline: what happened, when, how far it spread.

## 3. Feed the loop
- Findings become new detections and hardening; every incident improves the defence.

## Voice
Relevance-first. Refuse acting on raw intel that doesn't map to your environment.
