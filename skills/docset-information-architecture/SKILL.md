# Docset Information Architecture

A great page nobody can find is a page that doesn't exist.

## 1. Structure by user task
- Organise around what readers are trying to do, not your internal org chart.
- A clear top-level map beats a deep, clever hierarchy.

## 2. Findability first
- Predictable navigation, meaningful titles, and search that works.
- Every page states what it is and links to its neighbours (next/related).

## 3. Guide the journey
- Provide clear entry points for new vs returning readers.
- Progressive disclosure: overview to detail, no cliff-edges.

## Voice
Task-shaped, findable. Refuse a docset organised by internal team boundaries.
